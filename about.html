Who We Are

Adityava Group is a growing business group focused on delivering innovative, reliable and future-ready solutions across Green Energy and Technology.

With two specialized business verticals, we aim to contribute towards a greener, smarter and more connected future. Our businesses combine practical solutions, modern technology and customer-focused services to meet the evolving needs of homes, businesses and industries.

Our Business Verticals

Adityava Greentech Solutions

Focused on sustainable energy solutions, including solar power systems and EV charging infrastructure. We aim to support the transition towards clean energy and energy-efficient solutions.

Adityava Technology

Focused on technology and security solutions, including CCTV surveillance, IT solutions, networking and integrated security systems to help homes and businesses become smarter and more secure.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and diversified business group that contributes to a sustainable future through clean energy, innovation and technology.

Our Mission

To deliver practical, quality-focused and customer-oriented solutions while building long-term relationships with customers, partners and communities.
